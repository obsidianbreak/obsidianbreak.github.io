# Art direction

One idea: a coffee house, by day and by night. The board never changes (cream latte tiles against roast brown tiles, a dark ball and a gold ball). Only the world around it changes between Day and Night.

## Palette

| Token | Day | Night | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ground | `#f4ecd8` paper | `#1a0b03` espresso | page and status bar area |
| Panel | cream at 88% | `#271609` at 88% | cards and sheets |
| Text | `#2a1809` | `#f6eacc` cream | main text |
| Muted text | brown at 62% | `#bdb09a` | secondary text |
| Accent | `#d9654f` coral | `#d9a767` caramel | "vs", progress, focus |
| Primary button | espresso on cream | cream with espresso text | main action |
| Tiles | `#f6eacc` and `#52290e` | same | the game, identical in both modes |
| Balls | `#1b0f07` and `#e3b95f` | same | player and opponent |

Night colours were derived in OKLCH from the Day hues (brown 55, caramel 72, cream 88) so the two themes feel like one place at different hours.

## Contrast (WCAG 2.2)

| Pair | Ratio |
| --- | --- |
| Cream text on espresso ground | 16.1 to 1 |
| Cream on night panel | 14.6 to 1 |
| Secondary text on night panel | 10.6 to 1 |
| Muted text on night panel | 7.0 to 1 |
| Caramel on espresso | 8.9 to 1 |
| Espresso text on cream button | 16.0 to 1 |
| Cream tile against roast tile | 10.4 to 1 |
| Dark ball on cream tile | 15.7 to 1 |
| Gold ball on roast tile | 6.8 to 1 |

Roast tiles sit close to the night ground (1.5 to 1), so the board gets a thin cream outline and a soft caramel glow in Night mode.

## Foam background

Night uses espresso paper, caramel lines at half strength, and one warm glow high on the screen, like a lamp over the counter.
